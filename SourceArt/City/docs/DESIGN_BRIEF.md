# Breed — City Foundation Design Brief

## Game
Stylized single-player / multiplayer game. An incident at the city **zoo** (explosion / major
failure) lets many animals escape across the city. The player starts with a **small pet shop**,
explores the city, finds and captures escaped animals (small ones easy; big ones need teamwork
or tools), brings them back, cares for / sells / breeds them, and grows the shop into a large
business with new facilities.

## Tone & style
- Stylized / semi-stylized, colorful but tasteful, cozy, playful, slightly chaotic (multiplayer).
- Grounded enough to feel like a believable small **American-style** town/city.
- NOT photoreal, NOT childish/toy-like, NOT cheap ultra-low-poly. Clean forms, slightly rounded.
- Readable from gameplay camera distances.

## City direction
- NOT New York; NOT a skyscraper grid. A **medium-sized, organic, slightly messy, interesting** town.
- Winding / organic streets, natural (non-grid) intersections, some narrow roads, some open roads.
- A small **downtown** feeling in one area (a little denser, a few 3-5 storey buildings at most).
- **Residential / suburban** streets elsewhere (houses, yards, cul-de-sacs, townhouses).
- **Park** areas, **utility / service / back-alley** areas, a **canal / river / pond**.
- Places where animals could hide or roam; fun to explore on foot or small vehicle.
- Mild elevation variation only where it helps (e.g. a park hill); keep it manageable.
- Footprint ~ **700 m x 700 m** (flexible; exploration flow > exact size). Unity-friendly.

## Required districts / places
1. **Starting Pet Shop area** — the player's home base. Small at first, central-ish, easy to
   understand and return to, with **room around it to expand** (side lot / back yard / adjacent plot).
2. **Zoo / zoo-adjacent area** — where the incident starts. Recognizable landmark, meaningful.
3. **Park / green space** — walking paths, open lawns, tree clusters; animals roam here.
4. **Downtown / commercial** core, **residential** neighborhoods, **service / warehouse / backstreet**
   area, **water** (canal/river/pond).
5. **Gameplay-friendly spaces**: hidden corners, alleyways, open lots, yards, back areas.

## Gameplay-aware rules
- Enjoyable movement, exploration, hidden + open space, handcrafted (not random) feel.
- Dense enough to be interesting, not so dense navigation is frustrating.
- Pet shop central to progression. Supports solo + multiplayer.
- Leave room for: vehicles, NPC pedestrians, customer traffic, animal habitats, capture tools,
  shop expansion, breeding rooms, future interiors.
